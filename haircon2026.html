How it works
01

Customer scans your QR

A QR code sits on your mirror or counter. After their cut, customers scan it and register in under 30 seconds. No app. No account to create.

02

We track their cycle

Every customer has their own haircut frequency. We track where they are in that cycle and know exactly when they're due, without you having to think about it.

03

They get a reminder

When they're due, we send a short personal SMS in your name. No marketing language. Just: you're due, your barber's in, walk in whenever you're ready.

What you get

Automated reminders

SMS reminders sent every morning to customers who are due, overdue, or lapsed. Completely automatic, with no daily effort required from you.

Holiday-aware

Going away? Add your dates to the dashboard. We'll pull reminders forward before you leave and let customers know when you're back.

Barber dashboard

See every registered customer, their reminder status, and last visit date. Clean, fast, works on any device. Log in from your phone between cuts.

Built-in opt-out

Every message includes Reply STOP. Customers who opt out are removed instantly and permanently. Fully compliant with UK messaging regulations.
