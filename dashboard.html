How This Works
— Customer is registered directly into WalkIn.
— If you enter their last visit date, the reminder engine will calculate where they are in their cycle immediately.
— If no last visit date is entered, they'll be treated as due and receive a reminder on the next send.
— They'll receive a message from you when they're due — same as any QR-registered customer.
— You can always remove a customer by contacting support.
