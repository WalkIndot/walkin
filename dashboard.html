How This Works
Customer is registered directly into WalkIn. — no QR scan needed.
If you enter their last visit date, the reminder engine calculates where they are in their cycle immediately.
If no last visit date is entered, they'll be treated as due and receive a reminder on the next send.
They'll receive messages the same way as any QR-registered customer.
To remove a customer, contact support.
